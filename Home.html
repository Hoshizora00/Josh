Binangkal Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 500 grams all purpose flour
  • 200 grams white sugar
  • 2 tps baking powder
  • 1 tps baking soda
  • 150 ml evaporated milk
  • 2 tbps oil
  • 1 tbps vanilla essense or vanilla extract
  • cooking oil for deep frying
  • 1 cup sesame seeds
  • 500 grams all purpose flour

Procedure

  • 1. Combine flour, baking powder, baking soda and sugar. Mix well.
  • 2. In a different bowl, combine oil, milk and vanilla extract.
  • 3. Combine the dry mixture and the liquid mixture together. Mix throughly.
  • 4. Using two tablespoons, make a roundball and roll it in a sesame seeds.
  • 5. Then, using the palm of your hands, roll it again.
  • 6. Heat the oil in medium heat.
  • 7. Deep fry the formed batter balls.
  • 8. Drain excess oil
  • 9. Serve and ENJOY!
